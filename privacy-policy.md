# Privacy Policy — POLYDEPT Bot

**Last Updated:** October 13, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dimas Bot ("the Bot") handles information when users interact with it on Discord.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, Dimas Bot may process the following information:

- **Discord User IDs:** To identify users who interact with commands.
- **Server and Channel IDs:** To identify where commands are used and perform requested server management actions.
- **Command Inputs:** To process commands submitted by users.
- **Message Data:** If required by enabled moderation features, the Bot may process message content or message metadata to perform the requested action.

The Bot only collects or retains information that is necessary for its stated functionality.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

- Execute bot commands.
- Perform server moderation and message management.
- Maintain the Bot's functionality, security, and reliability.
- Troubleshoot errors and respond to user requests.

Information will not be used for unrelated purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the Bot's functionality or meet applicable legal requirements.

If the Bot does not permanently store certain information, that information may still be processed temporarily while a command is executed.

## 5. Data Sharing

Dimas Bot does not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the Bot, comply with legal obligations, protect security, or when otherwise permitted by applicable law.

## 6. Data Deletion Requests

Users may contact the Bot owner to request access to or deletion of personal information stored by the Bot.

Requests can be submitted through Discord by contacting the Bot owner. Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

## 7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the Bot. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Dimas Bot operates through Discord. Use of Discord is also subject to [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the owner of Dimas Bot through Discord.
